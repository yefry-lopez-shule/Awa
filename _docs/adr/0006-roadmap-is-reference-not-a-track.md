# The self-study roadmap is reference material, not a second track

The student maintains a spreadsheet mapping UNED courses to their Harvard,
Stanford, MIT and Caltech equivalents, with a hand-written note on what each UNED
course leaves uncovered. An early scope treated this as a parallel curriculum with
its own weekly hour budget, competing with UNED for study time.

It isn't one. Roadmap entries are read-only reference attached to a Course: they
are never studied, scheduled, recommended, or given hours. The spreadsheet remains
the place they are curated; the app imports a flat list.

## Consequences

- Two features that existed only to serve the second track were cut: the
  surplus-hours allocation rule, and a separate self-study streak.
- The streak survived, redefined as days on which *any* study was logged — where
  it does more useful work as an alarm for logging going stale.
- The UNED plan is the spine of the data model. Roadmap entries hang off courses,
  which inverts the spreadsheet, where the roadmap is the spine and UNED is a
  column.
